A companies information enriched dataset sample of 1000 company records. Dataset was extracted using the <b>Bright Data API</b>.

<h2>Some of the data points that are included in the dataset:</h2>

* ```url```: URL associated with the company record
* ```id_lc```: Company identifier
* ```name_lc```: Name of the company
* ```country_code_lc```: Country code associated with the company
* ```locations_lc```: Company locations
* ```followers_lc```: Number of followers on LinkedIn
* ```employees_in_linkedin_lc```: Number of employees associated with the company on LinkedIn
* ```about_lc```: Company overview and description

And a lot more.

This is a sample subset which is derived from the "Companies Information Enriched Dataset"
which includes more than <b>30,400,000 company records</b>.

Available dataset file formats: <b>JSON, NDJSON, JSON Lines, CSV, or Parquet. Optionally, files can be compressed to .gz</b>.

Dataset delivery type options: <b>Email, API download, Webhook, Amazon S3, Google Cloud storage, Google Cloud PubSub, Microsoft Azure, Snowflake, SFTP</b>.

Update frequency: <b>Once, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Biannual, or Custom basis</b>.

Data enrichment available as an addition to the data points extracted: <b>Based on request.</b>

<h2>What are the Companies Information Enriched dataset use cases?</h2>

<h3>1. Company research and competitor mapping</h3>
Businesses use enriched company records to research organizations, understand their activities and map their geographic presence. Comparing company descriptions, locations and LinkedIn employee counts helps identify relevant competitors and build a clearer picture of the market.

<h3>2. Account discovery and CRM enrichment</h3>
Sales and business development teams use company names, descriptions and locations to identify relevant accounts and add context to existing CRM records. Company identifiers and source URLs support record matching and help teams organize their account research.

<h3>3. Workforce and company visibility tracking</h3>
Analysts compare LinkedIn employee counts and follower counts across companies. With recurring dataset updates, they can track changes in workforce representation and company visibility on LinkedIn to support market research and account prioritization.
